# Contextual Memory Assistant for the Visually Impaired

**An AI-powered assistive system designed to enhance the independence of visually impaired individuals through environmental awareness, contextual understanding, and persistent memory.**

---

## Overview

The Contextual Memory Assistant is an AI-powered assistive system aimed at improving the daily experiences and independence of visually impaired individuals.

Existing assistive technologies primarily focus on helping users perceive and understand their immediate surroundings. This project extends beyond real-time environmental awareness by introducing the concept of **persistent contextual memory**, enabling the system to retain and recall information about previously observed environments, objects, and their locations.

The system aims to develop a contextual understanding of the user's surroundings over time, allowing relevant information to be retrieved through natural language and voice-based interactions.

Users can interact with the assistant using queries such as:

- Where did I last keep my keys?
- When was my wallet last observed?
- Where was my water bottle previously located?
- What objects were present on my study table?

By combining environmental perception with persistent memory and intelligent information retrieval, the system aims to provide a more personalized and context-aware assistive experience.

## Our Vision

To develop an intelligent assistive companion that enables visually impaired individuals to build a better understanding of their surroundings, retain contextual information, and independently recall previously observed details whenever required.

## Key Highlights

- **Environmental Awareness:** Understands and gathers relevant information about the user's surrounding environment.
- **Contextual Memory:** Maintains information about previously observed objects, locations, and environmental details.
- **Intelligent Information Retrieval:** Enables users to retrieve relevant contextual information through natural language queries.
- **Voice-Based Interaction:** Facilitates accessible, hands-free communication through voice commands and audio responses.
- **Continuous Contextual Understanding:** Builds and maintains an evolving understanding of the user's environment over time.

## Project Concept

The central idea of this project is to extend assistive technology from immediate environmental perception to persistent contextual understanding.

Rather than simply identifying objects and describing the current surroundings, the proposed system aims to retain relevant observations, establish relationships between objects and their environments, and make previously acquired information accessible when needed.

This approach introduces a memory-oriented dimension to assistive technology, enabling more meaningful interactions between users and their surroundings.

## Future Scope

The project provides a foundation for developing more advanced and personalized assistive technologies. Future enhancements may include improved environmental understanding, adaptive memory capabilities, enhanced contextual reasoning, and integration with wearable devices to support a wider range of everyday activities.
